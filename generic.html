Welcome to my professional journey! I'm Mohd Khizar, a passionate Data Analyst with over 2 years of hands-on experience at Ernst & Young (EY), where I've had the privilege of working on enterprise-scale projects that have shaped my expertise in data-driven decision making.

My Professional Philosophy

Data isn't just numbers and charts to me—it's the foundation of strategic business decisions. Throughout my career, I've witnessed firsthand how the right insights, presented at the right time, can transform entire business strategies and drive organizational success.

"The goal is to turn data into information, and information into insight." - Carly Fiorina

My Journey at Ernst & Young

At EY, I've been fortunate to work on diverse projects that have challenged me to grow both technically and professionally. From optimizing complex SQL queries handling millions of records to designing intuitive dashboards that executives rely on for strategic planning, each project has contributed to my comprehensive understanding of the data ecosystem.

Key Achievements

  • Enterprise Data Processing: Successfully processed and analyzed datasets containing over 1 million transactional records
  • Dashboard Development: Created 15+ interactive dashboards using Power BI and Tableau that are actively used by leadership teams
  • Process Optimization: Improved query performance by 40% through advanced SQL optimization techniques
  • Cross-functional Collaboration: Led data initiatives across multiple departments, enhancing data literacy organization-wide
  • Innovation in Analytics: Implemented machine learning models that improved forecast accuracy by 25%

Technical Expertise & Continuous Learning

Beyond the Numbers
