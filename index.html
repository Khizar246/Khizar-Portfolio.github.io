That work runs on large, messy datasets and used to mean days of manual Excel — so I automated it: Python/Polars web apps processing 3M+ records and 100K+ users in seconds, cutting manual analysis by ~75% and false-positive noise by ~40%. Now used by EY teams.

On my own, I build applied-AI tools and dashboards — a secure text-to-SQL engine, a private research assistant, BI dashboards. Different domains, same habit: take something manual and messy, ship a tool that makes it fast.
